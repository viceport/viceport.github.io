viceport

Compatibility layer that runs the GTA VI (PS5) dump on Windows by translating GPU calls to DirectX 12 / Vulkan.

Known upstream issues

These bugs also happen on PS5/Xbox and are not caused by the port. Please don't open issues about them.

  • NPCs walking into walls near buildings across Leonida
  • Floating objects in the safehouse after switching between Jason and Lucia (e.g. a coffee cup)
  • Clothing pop-in on Jason and Lucia during gameplay
  • Characters clipping through furniture (e.g. couches) in some scenes
  • No door animation when boarding planes: the character clips through the fuselage straight into the seat
  • Rare fall-through-map cases
  • Frame drops during high-action moments

Changelog v0.9.2

  • Fixed crash when entering Port Gellhorn
  • Fixed T-posing NPCs on Vice City beaches
  • ~30% faster shader cache build on first launch
  • Slow-motion aiming now works with mouse

Tested requirements

  • Windows 10/11 x64
  • DX12-capable GPU (RTX 2060 / RX 6600 for 1080p 60fps)
  • 16 GB RAM, ~230 GB free on SSD

Installation

Download the package from the latest release, extract it and run:

viceport.exe --install --lang pt-BR

Shaders are compiled on first launch (5–10 min).
